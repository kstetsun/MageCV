How to Play

You are a freshly graduated mage seeking your first post in the magical world. Send resumes each day, charm the locals, and keep going — victory will come.

The Daily Loop ▼

Each day you have a limited number of resumes to send. Use them wisely — then press Next Day to advance. NPC encounters and limits also reset each new day.

Demo: 5 resumes / day Long: 10 resumes / day
Building a Resume ▼

Every resume is assembled from three elements: a plant decoration, a profile photo, and a work start date. A job card tells you what the employer wants — read it carefully before choosing.

Each correct choice helps your cause; each wrong one works against you. You will receive qualitative feedback after submitting — no numbers, just feeling.

3 elements per resume 25 job cards
NPCs & Encounters ▼

Three characters may appear at any time with a countdown timer of 15–45 seconds. Miss the timer and they leave without effect. Speak with them for buffs, debuffs, or surprises.

The Elf Networking enthusiast. Tends toward helpful buffs when engaged sincerely.
The Dwarf Veteran realist. Results are modest — rarely a big bonus, rarely a disaster.
The Wizard Utterly unpredictable. Always applies a random modifier regardless of what you say.

Winning ▼

There is no way to lose — only to keep trying. At some point, when you have impressed the right people enough, an offer will arrive. You will not know how close you are. Stay persistent. The offer letter awaits.
