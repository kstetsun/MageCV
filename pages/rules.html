How to Play

You are a recent graduate of a magical academy, seeking your first job in the fantasy world. Send out resumes every day until you land your dream job!

The Daily Loop

Each day, you can send a limited number of resumes. When you reach the daily limit, press the "Next Day" button to continue your job search on a new day.

Resume Building

Each resume has three elements: a plant, a photo, and a date. Your choices affect your score — experiment to find the best combinations!

NPCs

Randomly, magical NPCs may appear: the friendly Elf, the realistic Dwarf, and the unpredictable Wizard. They can give you helpful buffs or tricky debuffs, so be ready!

Modes

  • Demo: 5 resumes per day, fixed win target.
  • Long: 10 resumes per day, random win target.

Win Condition

Keep sending resumes and improving your score. When you reach the hidden win target, you'll know — victory is magical!
